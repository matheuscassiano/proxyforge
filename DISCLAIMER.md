## Card builder

Every card made in the card builder carries a "PROXY · NOT FOR SALE" mark inside the cut line. It can be moved and resized, but not removed. PNGs saved from the builder are also tagged as proxies in their metadata.

The builder's asset packs (frames, symbols and fonts for Magic, Pokémon and Flesh and Blood) are **not part of ProxyForge**. The repository only holds manifests with the public URLs of community projects (Card Conjurer, pokecardmaker.net, Scryfall's symbol SVGs). The files are downloaded to your computer when you use them or press "Download pack", and they stay there. Those projects do not state a licence for these files, and the underlying artwork and typefaces belong to their owners. Downloading and using them is your responsibility, for personal use only.
